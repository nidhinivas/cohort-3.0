ABOUT

Holistic health is an approach to healthcare that

considers the whole person-body, mind, spirit, and

emotions-in the pursuit of optimal health and

wellness. It emphasizes the interconnectedness of these

aspects and seek to address imbalances or

disharmonies that may arise with them. Holistic health

practitioners may include naturopathic doctors,

integrative medicine physicians, holistics nurses,

chiropractors, acupuncturists, nutritionists, and various

types of therapists (such as massage therapists,

psychotherapists, and energy healers).
